01 · Decision brief

Strong foundations.
Several resident services remain to build.

The project already covers core society administration—residents and units, finance, vendors, staff attendance, reports and community tools. The largest opportunity is extending those foundations into convenient, resident-facing services.

01

Administration is established

Resident/unit management, finance workflows, vendors and staff attendance have corresponding dashboard areas.

02

Community tools can be extended

Groups, events, RSVPs and classifieds offer a base for activities and property listings, but do not cover every requested workflow.

03

Marketplace services are the main gap

Dedicated grocery, pharmacy, home-service, group-buy and advertiser journeys were not identified.

04

Trust and finance must underpin revenue

Partner verification, consent, refunds, settlement records and transparent reporting should precede monetization.

Business opportunitiesResident services · points 10–13
03 · Marketplace opportunities

Every service needs a complete journey

A marketplace is more than a list of providers: residents need to request or order a service, track what happens, get support and understand charges.

10 · Society grocery ordering

MISSING

Meaning: Residents order groceries from participating local stores through the app. Delivery records are not grocery shopping.

Add: Resident app → Marketplace → Groceries: store/product catalogue, cart, order status, delivery or pickup, payment, cancellation/refund and support. Admin → Partners: store onboarding and order oversight.

Revenue: Commission is an agreed share of eligible sales. Define calculation, settlement timing and refund adjustments before launch; do not assume a rate.

11 · Medicine delivery

MISSING

Meaning: Residents request medicines from partner pharmacies and follow fulfillment/delivery status.

Add: Resident app → Pharmacy: pharmacy directory, order request, fulfillment updates and support; Admin → Pharmacy Partners: onboarding and order monitoring. Treat prescriptions and medicine details as sensitive, require consent and restrict access.

Guardrail: The app should not diagnose or give medical advice. Clearly define pharmacist responsibility, order validation, cancellations and any referral/payment arrangement.

12 · Home maintenance packages

RELATED TOOLS

Meaning: Residents book household services such as plumbing or electrical repair, potentially from fixed packages.

Now: Helpdesk, society asset maintenance and vendor tools are related, but they do not establish a private in-home booking workflow.

Add: Resident app → Home Services: service catalogue, appointment, quote, job status, completion, feedback and payment. Keep resident work separate from society-owned asset work orders.

13 · Rental and resale leads

CLASSIFIEDS BASE

Meaning: A lead is an interested person who has agreed to be contacted; it is not a completed rental or sale.

Now: Community Classifieds supports general listing details such as title, description, category, price and contact information.

Add: Community → Property Listings: rent/sale type, unit details, availability, owner authorization, moderation, inquiry consent and lead status. Protect contact details and track inquiries separately from listings.

Shared foundation: Partner onboarding, resident consent, order/inquiry history, support handling and a finance ledger should be designed once and reused across services.

Evidence: Community modules include Events/RSVPs and Classifieds; delivery types include grocery classification for gate deliveries, which is not an ordering workflow.

Proposals are new resident-facing workflows unless stated otherwise03 / 06
Business opportunitiesPartner services · points 14–17
04 · Monetization and trust

Build trust before monetizing

These opportunities involve advertising, identity documents, payments or referrals. Make terms visible, minimize sensitive data and record approvals.

14 · Local advertising

MISSING

Meaning: Nearby businesses pay for a clearly defined advertising period or placement.

Add: Admin → Advertisers: business profile, campaign dates, placement, subscription invoice and basic views/clicks. Resident app → labelled “Sponsored” offers. Define targeting boundaries and renewal/cancellation terms; a subscription is recurring payment for an agreed period.

15 · Domestic help verification

STAFF RECORDS ONLY

Meaning: Register and review household workers so residents and gate staff can verify approved access.

Now: Staff records/documents may cover employees, but are not a dedicated resident domestic-help verification flow.

Add: Staff & Visitors → Domestic Help: registration, document-review status, resident reference, expiry and gate pass. Restrict identity documents; show only necessary details. Any fee needs an approved, disclosed policy.

16 · Bulk purchasing

MISSING

Meaning: Residents combine demand to seek a group price or discount. Society procurement is for society purchases, not automatically a resident group buy.

Add: Resident Marketplace → Group Buys: product, minimum quantity, closing date, final price, payment/refund terms, delivery and order status. Admin manages campaigns and supplier commitments. State clearly what happens if the minimum quantity is not met.

17 · Society insurance offers

VENDOR/DOCUMENT BASE

Meaning: An insurer or broker provides an offer, and residents may choose to request contact or a referral.

Now: Insurance vendor categories or asset insurance documents do not provide resident offer comparison/referral tracking.

Add: Admin → Insurance Partners; Resident app → Insurance Offers: provider details, offer terms, opt-in request and referral tracking. Disclose partnership terms; do not imply personalized financial advice.

Revenue rules to decide before launch: who pays, what event earns revenue, how refunds/cancellations affect it, how partners are settled, what the society records, and how residents are told about fees or referrals.

Evidence: Vendor categories include insurance and asset documents can include insurance records; neither constitutes a resident-facing insurance partnership. Staff documents are not a substitute for a purpose-built, permissioned verification flow.

Product planPlacement, delivery and success measures
05 · Where it fits

Extend existing areas; add services deliberately

Community

Kids’ activities, sports/cultural events, property listings and senior-focused community programs. Reuse groups, events, RSVPs and classifieds where they fit.

Resident app

Expose activities, support requests and marketplace orders in resident-facing sections with notifications, status tracking and support contacts.

Finance & Reports

Keep income, expenses, vendor invoices, commissions/referrals and settlements traceable to the correct period and source record.

Partners & operations

Use vendor management for partner onboarding. Add service-specific controls for stores, pharmacies, advertisers, home-service providers and insurers.

Residents & Units

Maintain one flat-linked resident source of truth. Reuse permissions and household relationships rather than copying resident records into new modules.

Staff & Visitors

Extend staff operations carefully for domestic-help registration, reviewed status and gate access; isolate sensitive identity documents.

Recommended sequence

PHASE 1

Strengthen foundations. Verify finance statements and audit history; improve event categories, classified property fields and partner records.

PHASE 2

Add community support. Pilot kids’ activities, opt-in senior support and richer event registration using existing community areas.

PHASE 3

Pilot one marketplace service. Select one service (for example, home maintenance or grocery ordering), onboard a few partners and validate the full support/refund/accounting cycle.

PHASE 4

Expand only after learning. Add pharmacy, group buying, advertising, domestic-help verification and insurance referrals with dedicated privacy, settlement and disclosure controls.

Measure whether pilots work

Resident valueAdoption, completion rate, satisfaction and resolution time.
Operational healthPartner response, cancellations, refunds and support load.
Financial clarityGross order value, fees due, refunds and reconciled settlements.

Recommended dependencies: permissions and consent → partner review → resident request/order → fulfillment/status → support/refund → finance reconciliation and reporting.

06 · Terms made simple

Glossary

Flat-wise resident database
A record linking each flat/unit to its residents, ownership or occupancy details.

Income and expenditure
Money the society receives and money it spends over a chosen period.

Digital accounting
Recording transactions, balances and financial reports in software instead of relying only on paper or disconnected spreadsheets.

Audit report
A structured review of financial records and supporting evidence, intended to help check accuracy and accountability.

Financial transparency
Giving authorized people a clear, understandable view of society finances and the records behind them.

Vendor
A business or person supplying goods or services to the society or its residents.

Commission
An agreed fee or share associated with a completed eligible sale or service; the rate and rules must be decided.

Pharmacy partnership
An arrangement with a pharmacy to fulfill resident medicine requests under agreed service and privacy rules.

Service-booking revenue
Income earned from a service booking, such as a disclosed platform fee or provider commission.

Rental/resale lead
A person who has expressed interest in renting or buying and agreed to be contacted; not a completed deal.

Monthly advertiser subscription
A recurring payment for agreed advertising placement during a defined month or period.

Domestic-help verification
A controlled process for registering and reviewing information about a household worker for approved society access.

Bulk purchasing / group buying
Combining several residents’ orders to negotiate or qualify for a shared price or discount.

Referral partnership
An arrangement where the society directs an interested resident to a provider under disclosed terms.

The platform has a strong administrative base. The next product step is to turn selected community and partner opportunities into complete, transparent resident journeys—starting small, measuring results and expanding only when service, privacy and accounting controls are ready.
